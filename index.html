Methodology and important limitations
  • The starting federal basic exclusion is $15 million per donor for 2026. Exclusion growth is an editable, smooth-compounding assumption—not a forecast of actual IRS annual amounts.
  • Modeled gift capacity equals the lesser of current assets and the starting exclusion less entered prior taxable gifts. No gift tax, annual gift exclusions, valuation discounts, generation-skipping transfer tax, or deceased-spouse unused exclusion is modeled.
  • Estimated federal tax equals the selected marginal rate multiplied by the positive excess of the projected estate, plus prior taxable gifts and the modeled current gift, over the projected basic exclusion. This is a marginal-rate approximation—not the graduated unified tax and credit calculation.
  • In the retain scenario, all current assets grow inside the estate. In the gift scenario, the original gift and its growth are outside the estate, while the gift uses exemption. The same constant return applies to both. No additional contributions, distributions, spending, or fees are modeled.
  • Prior taxable gifts are held constant for exemption accounting in both scenarios. They are not grown or included in displayed family wealth. “Family wealth” therefore refers only to the assets entered as the current estate and the modeled gift from those assets.
  • Couple mode is a pooled proxy assuming equal allocation of assets, current gifts, and prior gifts, with both exclusions available at the same future horizon. It does not model differing death dates, portability elections, marital deductions, trust funding, or survivor taxation.
  • Gifts are assumed completed with no retained interests causing estate inclusion. Sufficient exemption is available and no gift tax is payable.
  • State taxes, income taxes, carryover basis versus a potential basis adjustment at death, and future law changes are excluded. These can materially change the overall benefit. Negative returns may produce negative modeled savings.
  • All dollar outputs are nominal. Exclusion growth is distinct from converting results into inflation-adjusted purchasing power.
  • Each chart point is a separate hypothetical transfer-tax calculation at that horizon. The chart does not assume estate tax is paid each year.
  • Results are educational illustrations, not predictions or individualized recommendations. Independent tax, legal, calculation, and applicable compliance review is needed before public distribution.
Calculation formulas

Projected basic exclusion = $15,000,000 × number of donors × (1 + exclusion growth assumption)years.

Retain scenario estate = current estate × (1 + asset return)years.

Gift scenario estate = (current estate − current gift) × (1 + asset return)years.

Projected gifted assets = current gift × (1 + asset return)years.

Modeled federal tax = max(0, projected estate + prior taxable gifts + modeled current gift − projected basic exclusion) × assumed marginal tax rate. The modeled current gift is zero in the retain scenario.

Projected federal estate-tax reduction = retain-scenario federal tax − gift-scenario federal tax.

Projected family wealth = projected estate + projected gifted assets − modeled federal estate tax.
