Scope, assumptions, and limitations
  • One New York resident donor only. No married-couple toggle: this version does not model marital deductions, separate ownership, QTIP, trusts, first and second deaths, or portability. It does not determine residency or filing obligations.
  • All current estate assets are assumed includible for New York purposes. No expenses, debts, charitable deductions, outside-New-York real or tangible property, or other return adjustments are modeled.
  • The modeled gift is assumed completed while a New York resident, of property subject to the addback rule, with no retained interests causing estate inclusion. No other prior gifts or addbacks are modeled. No gift tax is paid. Verify available federal exemption separately.
  • For death before January 1, 2032, the original taxable gift amount is added to the modeled New York taxable estate if death occurs on or before the third anniversary of the gift. The exact third anniversary is conservatively included; confirm boundary dates with counsel. Appreciation is not added back. Deaths on or after January 1, 2032 receive no addback under this model of the currently scheduled expiration.
  • All entered assets grow at the same constant rate from the gift date to death, using actual days divided by 365.2425. No spending, distributions, additional gifts, expenses, income tax, or law changes are modeled.
  • The 2026 exclusion is $7,350,000. Future exclusions use the entered annual growth assumption for each calendar year after 2026, without statutory rounding. Rates and bracket thresholds use the current ET-706 table and are held constant.
  • At or below the exclusion, modeled tax is zero. Between 100% and 105% of the exclusion, the credit is the table tax on the reduced exclusion amount specified by the ET-706 instructions—not simply a linear reduction of the full credit. The worksheet ratio is rounded to four decimal places for this estimate. Above 105%, no exclusion credit is allowed.
  • Family wealth includes only current estate assets and the gift from those assets, less New York estate tax. It excludes federal estate tax, gift tax, income taxes, carryover basis versus a potential basis adjustment at death, and all other taxes. Negative returns can increase New York tax under an addback scenario.
  • All projected dollar amounts are nominal. Exclusion growth does not adjust the results for inflation or purchasing power.
  • Educational prototype only. No automatic legal updates. Independently verify calculations, current law, assumptions, and applicable compliance requirements before distributing. Not tax, legal, investment, or insurance advice.
